GeoTrace privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026. GeoTrace is a free Android app for recording GPS objects, paths, and plant photos. Contact: mathias@matricula3d.com.br.

The app does not show advertising and does not sell personal data. It does not include an analytics or advertising SDK.

Location

While you record, the app reads precise GPS (latitude, longitude, altitude, and accuracy). Those points stay on the phone until you choose Sync. Sync sends them to app.matricula3d.com.br for the account that owns the key you pasted. The app does not read location in the background.

Photos

Photos you take stay on the phone. Sync can upload them with the object they belong to. When you tap Identify, the selected photos and their coordinate are sent to xAI at api.x.ai, using the API key you entered, so the plant can be named. The answer is saved on the phone with that coordinate.

Keys stored on the phone

The account key, an optional website password, and the xAI key are stored only on the phone. The account key is sent only to app.matricula3d.com.br. The xAI key is sent only to api.x.ai when you identify a plant. App backup of this data is turned off.

Accounts

GeoTrace does not create an account. Accounts are created on the website. To delete what is on the phone, clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. To delete data already synced, remove it in your website account or email mathias@matricula3d.com.br.
